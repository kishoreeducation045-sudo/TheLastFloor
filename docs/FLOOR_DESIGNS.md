# THE LAST FLOOR – VACCINE PROTOCOL: FLOOR DESIGNS & TIMERS

## Detailed Floor Matrix

### Floor B1: Underground Parking Garage
- **Timer:** 2:00 (120 seconds)
- **Environment:** Low-ceiling concrete subterranean garage. South side holds the smashed heavy rescue truck pinned against a shattered column with sparks and fuel drips. North wall features the emergency elevator and digital keypad. West side has abandoned vehicles with hiding spots. East side has maintenance cages and locked emergency stairs.
- **Puzzle:** Four symbols painted onto pillars must be cross-referenced against a clipboard in the maintenance room to yield a 4-digit numeric code for the elevator keypad.
- **Enemies:** None (Story onboarding and environmental hazard introduction).

### Floor 1: The Infected Residents
- **Timer:** 3:12 (192 seconds)
- **Environment:** Dilapidated residential corridor with flickering overhead fluorescent strips, blood trails, and doors to Apartments 101, 102, 103, and 104.
- **Puzzle:** Collect 3 circuit fuses from apartment interiors, install them in the corridor distribution panel to restore power, and read a security PIN from a flickering CCTV monitor.
- **Enemies:** Resident (slow lurkers) and Screecher (crouches in dark corners; screams if approached).

### Floor 2: The Cold Clinic
- **Timer:** 4:24 (264 seconds)
- **Environment:** Abandoned urgent care facility with patient triage, operating rooms, and cryogenic medicine freezers.
- **Puzzle:** Adjust 3 refrigeration dials to match target temperatures noted on patient charts scattered in examination rooms.
- **Enemy:** Exploder (bloated specimen that stalks players and detonates upon close contact).

### Floor 3: The Power Control Floor
- **Timer:** 5:36 (336 seconds)
- **Environment:** High-voltage transformer maze with humming electrical conduits and dark sub-tunnels.
- **Puzzle:** Throw circuit breakers in an exact randomized chronological sequence indicated by wire colors.
- **Enemy:** Crawler (slithers beneath low machinery and vents to target player legs).

### Floor 4: The Infected Ward
- **Timer:** 6:48 (408 seconds)
- **Environment:** Long hospital ward with partitioned bed curtains and discarded IV stands.
- **Puzzle:** Match 3 patient wristband identifiers to pharmaceutical dispensary lockers to obtain master keys.
- **Enemy:** Blind Hunter (eyeless infected that responds only to sprinting, jumping, and gunfire).

### Floor 5: The Research Lab Alpha
- **Timer:** 8:00 (480 seconds)
- **Environment:** High-tech research wing with bio-containment pods and centrifuge equipment.
- **Puzzle:** Reconstruct an 8-character DNA codon string on the master console using partial laboratory notes.
- **Enemy:** Runner (hyper-agile infected sprint chaser).

### Floor 6: The Haunted Wing
- **Timer:** 9:12 (552 seconds)
- **Environment:** Wealthy penthouse community lounge with vintage furniture, portrait gallery, and flickering chandeliers.
- **Puzzle:** Light 4 ceremonial talismans in an alignment pattern revealed by UV light.
- **Enemy:** Phantom (supernatural entity that teleports, flickers lights, and creates phantasms).

### Floor 7: The Maintenance Maze
- **Timer:** 10:24 (624 seconds)
- **Environment:** Industrial pipe network, hissing steam vents, and catwalks.
- **Puzzle:** Turn 4 steam valves to stabilize elevator shaft air pressure.
- **Enemy:** Brute (giant armor-plated infected capable of breaking barricades).

### Floor 8: The Isolation Block
- **Timer:** 11:36 (696 seconds)
- **Environment:** Maximum biohazard containment suites with observation mirrors.
- **Puzzle:** Collect 4 biometric keycards across quarantine cells to initiate decontamination.
- **Enemy:** Mutant (aggressive quad-pedal abomination with ceiling ambushes and acid spit).

### Floor 9: The Final Guardian
- **Timer:** 12:48 (768 seconds)
- **Environment:** Massive circular containment reactor chamber with observation decks.
- **Puzzle:** Manually activate 3 reactor cooling rods while kiting the boss.
- **Boss:** The Abomination (towering composite boss entity with ground pounds).
- **Secondary Enemy:** Wraith (spectral ghost phasing through walls).

### Floor 10: The Vaccine Protocol & Extraction
- **Timer:** 14:00 (840 seconds)
- **Environment:** Sterile cryogenic research vault with the central Vaccine Chamber.
- **Puzzle:** Insert the 3 researcher keys, calibrate the centrifuge, retrieve the prototype Vaccine vial, and signal the roof extraction beacon.
- **Final Encounter:** The Origin (patient zero entity). Escape back to the elevator or extraction point before the building collapses.
