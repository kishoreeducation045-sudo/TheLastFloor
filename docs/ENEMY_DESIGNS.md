# THE LAST FLOOR – VACCINE PROTOCOL: ENEMY & ENTITY DESIGN

## Regular Infected Roster (8 Types)

1. **Resident (Common Infected)**
   - *Behavior:* Shuffling, erratic patrol paths, low visual perception.
   - *Attack:* Swipes with infected arms (15 damage).
   - *Audio:* Shallow raspy groans, dragging feet.

2. **Screecher (Alarm Vector)**
   - *Behavior:* Stationary or low crawl in blind corners; startles upon direct flashlight beam or proximity.
   - *Attack:* Releases a high-decibel auditory scream that stuns nearby players for 1.5 seconds and alerts all infected within 60 studs.
   - *Audio:* Ear-piercing shriek, rapid panting.

3. **Crawler (Low-Angle Ambush)**
   - *Behavior:* Navigates beneath hospital beds, sofas, and ventilation ducts.
   - *Attack:* Ankle bit/claw (10 damage + 30% movement slow for 3 seconds).
   - *Audio:* Wet scuttling on tiles, guttural clicking.

4. **Runner (Sprint Hunter)**
   - *Behavior:* High sensory perception; sprints directly toward players at 22 studs/sec when line-of-sight is established.
   - *Attack:* Leaping pounce (25 damage).
   - *Audio:* Frantic heavy footsteps, aggressive snarling.

5. **Blind Hunter (Acoustic Stalker)**
   - *Behavior:* Eyes are atrophied and overgrown with fungal carapace. Unresponsive to flashlights or visual presence, but locks onto footsteps, jumping, and gunfire.
   - *Attack:* Devastating puncture strike (40 damage).
   - *Audio:* Deep throat clicks (echolocation), heavy wheezing.

6. **Brute (Armored Juggernaut)**
   - *Behavior:* Slow movement speed (8 studs/sec). Immune to light melee attacks. Can batter down barricaded wooden doors.
   - *Attack:* Heavy club swing (50 damage, sends player reeling backwards).
   - *Audio:* Metallic dragging sound, heavy thudding footsteps.

7. **Exploder (Biohazard Detonator)**
   - *Behavior:* Bloated with bioluminescent yellow pustules. Sprints toward squads once within 20 studs.
   - *Attack:* Self-destructs after a 1.5s swelling windup, spewing acid that deals 75 damage at epicenter and leaves an acidic cloud.
   - *Audio:* Gurgling fluid, rapid ticking before detonation.

8. **Mutant (Apex Hybrid)**
   - *Behavior:* Quadrupedal agile predator capable of traversing walls and dropping onto unsuspecting players.
   - *Attack:* Two-hit claw combo (30 damage) and projectile corrosive spit (15 damage).
   - *Audio:* Reptilian hiss, chitinous skittering.

---

## Supernatural Entities (2 Ghosts)

9. **Phantom (Spectral Trickster)**
   - *Behavior:* Phases in and out of optical visibility. Causes lights in the current room to flicker violently. Emits phantom directional footstep audio to confuse squad communication.
   - *Attack:* Teleports behind an isolated player and inflicts Paralyzing Chill (drains stamina to 0 and deals 20 psychic damage).
   - *Counterplay:* Staying grouped within flashlight halos dispels the Phantom.

10. **Wraith (Phase Stalker)**
    - *Behavior:* Does not respect physical partitions; glides directly through walls toward the player with the lowest health.
    - *Attack:* Tendril grasp that pulls the player away from their teammates.
    - *Counterplay:* Ultraviolet or flare gun exposure temporarily forces the Wraith to retreat into the ether.

---

## Boss Entities

- **The Abomination (Floor 9 Boss):**
  - Massive chimera created from merged containment specimens.
  - Attacks: Ground Slam (area of effect shockwave), Charge Attack (demolishes pillars), Sweeping Swipe.
  - Defeat Condition: Lure into cryogenic cooling rods and activate manual release valves to freeze the beast.

- **The Origin (Floor 10 Boss - Patient Zero):**
  - The patient zero organism rooted into the building's central ventilation and vaccine vault.
  - Attacks: Tendril whips, spore mine eruptions, summoning waves of Runners and Crawlers.
  - Defeat / Survival Condition: Calibrate the extraction centrifuge, synthesize the neutralizing anti-virus, inject the core biomass, and escape via the elevator/roof before building detonation.
